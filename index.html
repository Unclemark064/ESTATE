Frequently asked questions

How does Keyhold work?
Keyhold connects property owners and agents with buyers and renters directly. Owners post a listing with available inspection days; visitors book a convenient time slot. Everyone meets at the property — no middlemen required.
Is it free to list a property?
Yes, completely free. No listing fees, no commission, no subscription. Keyhold is a front-end demo — in a live product, listing will remain free for private owners.
Can I book multiple visits at once?
Yes. You can book visits to multiple properties and track all upcoming and past visits from the "My Visits" panel accessible via the navigation bar.
What if the owner or agent declines my visit?
If a visit is declined, the slot is released so you can book a new time. We recommend contacting the agent directly using the phone number shown on the listing. In a live product, you'd receive an instant notification for every status change.
Is Keyhold available outside Lagos?
This demo includes listings across Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt and Enugu. The full product will cover all major Nigerian cities, with plans to expand across West Africa.
How do I contact the agent directly?
Every listing shows the agent's name and phone number. After requesting a visit, you can reach them directly by phone or WhatsApp. All bookings are requests — the agent confirms before the visit is official.
